Privacy Policy

DIY 320 Drink Mix Calculator
Effective date: October 6, 2026

DIY 320 Drink Mix Calculator is a simple calculator for determining sugar and carbohydrate quantities in water. The app is designed to work locally on your device.

Information collection and use

The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive information. It does not require user accounts.

Values you enter are used only to perform calculations on your device. They remain local to the device and are not sent to the developer or any third party.

Device permissions

The app does not request device permissions.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, or similar technologies.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not collect or retain personal data, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct, or delete.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. Any revision will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.
